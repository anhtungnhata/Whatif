# "STEEL DAWN" — WWII Brick-Animated Short Film Script (LEGO style)

**Format:** 3D brick animation (toy-brick minifigures, everything built from plastic bricks)
**Aspect ratio:** 16:9 · **Frame rate:** 24 fps · **Runtime:** ~8:00
**Dialogue / Voice-over:** None — story told only through visuals, sound effects and music
**Setting:** Normandy coast, France — 6 June 1944 (D-Day), dawn to sunset
**Point of view:** A German defender (the character from the reference image)
**Tone:** Epic, tense, human and anti-war. A soldier caught in history's storm, not a glorification of any regime.
**Hard rules:** No swastikas, Nazi insignia or political symbols. No brand logos. No gore — minifigures fall or "pop apart," no blood.

---

## 1. CHARACTER SHEET

### Main character (from the reference image)
**Gefreiter KARL WEBER** — German infantryman, ~25, a farmer's son conscripted into the war.
- **LOOK (keep identical in every shot):** toy-brick minifigure; dark grey German steel helmet with dark chin strap; printed face with an angry, gritted-teeth expression, thick furrowed eyebrows, dirt smudges and small scratches; field-grey tunic with dark green collar; tan Y-strap suspenders; black ammo pouches on a black belt; small dark grey pouch on the chest; grey trousers with printed folds; dark, mud-splattered boots; dark brown bolt-action rifle with a long bayonet.
- **Signature detail:** a small photo tile of his mother and little sister in front of their farmhouse, kept inside his chest pocket.
- **Arc:** tough and hardened → overwhelmed and terrified → chooses mercy → survives and surrenders, glad to be alive.

### Supporting characters
| ID | Name | Role | LOOK |
|----|------|------|------|
| **G2** | **Feldwebel Otto Brandt** | Veteran sergeant, MG-42 gunner, Karl's mentor | Grey-haired printed face with moustache, tired eyes, helmet with chicken-wire foliage, MG-42 |
| **G3** | **Hans Richter** | 17-year-old recruit Karl protects | Small, pale, round face print with big scared eyes, oversized helmet, rifle too big for him |
| **G4** | Tiger tank commander | Brief ally in Act 3 | Black panzer uniform, headset, binoculars — plain grey Tiger tank, numbers only |
| **A1** | Sgt. Jack Reed | American squad leader (the "other side") | US helmet with net and photo tile in strap, stubble, Thompson SMG |
| **A2** | Pvt. Tommy Walsh | Young American soldier Karl spares | Freckles, oversized US helmet, M1 Garand |
| **A3** | Doc Bell | American medic who treats Hans at the end | Helmet with red crosses, armband, medic bag, matchstick in mouth |
| **A4** | Capt. Holloway | P-51 Mustang pilot | Flight helmet, goggles, plane "LUCKY LADY" |
| **EXTRAS** | German defenders, US soldiers, French villagers, a little French girl | Background | Period-accurate minifigures |

### Key vehicles / sets (all brick-built)
- **German:** concrete coastal bunker with firing slit, trench lines, Czech hedgehog obstacles, MG-42, a Tiger I tank, a Kübelwagen.
- **Allied:** invasion fleet, battleship, Higgins landing boats, B-17 bombers, P-51 Mustangs, M4 Sherman tanks.
- **Places:** cliff-top bunker above the beach → trenches → hedgerow lanes → French village with church tower → hill at sunset.

## 2. STORY STRUCTURE

| Part | Time | Title | Summary |
|------|------|-------|---------|
| Prologue | 0:00 – 0:40 | The Calm | Night in the bunker; fog lifts at dawn to reveal the invasion fleet |
| Act 1 | 0:40 – 2:10 | Thunder From the Sky | Bombers and battleships pound the coast; Karl digs Hans out of a collapsed trench |
| Act 2 | 2:10 – 4:40 | Hell on the Beach | The landing seen from the German side; Karl spares a young American; the bunker falls; Otto is lost |
| Act 3 | 4:40 – 6:50 | Iron and Fire | Retreat inland; village fight; a Tiger tank arrives and is destroyed; Karl and Hans are trapped |
| Epilogue | 6:50 – 8:00 | Steel Dawn | Surrender at sunset; the American medic saves Hans; two soldiers share their family photos |

**Seamless rule:** each scene hands off to the next through a match cut, a whip pan, an object passing the lens, or a sound bridge (marked **➜ TRANSITION**).

## 3. FULL SCRIPT

### PROLOGUE — "THE CALM" (0:00 – 0:40)

**SCENE 1 — Title (0:00 – 0:08)**
- **VISUAL:** Black. Sparks drift. Title "STEEL DAWN" (add in editor) forms from glowing brick pieces. Subtitle: *"Normandy, June 6, 1944."*
- **SFX:** Wind, distant waves, a slow dripping of water.
- **MUSIC:** Single low cello note, dark synth drone.
- **➜ TRANSITION:** Sparks become the glow of a candle inside the bunker.

**SCENE 2 — The bunker at night (0:08 – 0:28)**
- **VISUAL:** Inside the grey brick bunker, lit by a candle. **Karl** sits on an ammo crate, takes the photo of his mother and sister from his chest pocket, looks at it, puts it back. **Otto** cleans the MG-42 and hands Karl a tin cup of coffee. **Hans** sleeps curled up in a corner, his helmet too big. Karl places his own jacket over him.
- **CAMERA:** Slow dolly through the bunker; close-ups of the photo tile and faces.
- **SFX:** Candle crackle, metal clinks of the MG being cleaned, wind through the slit, Hans breathing softly.
- **MUSIC:** Soft, melancholic piano — the **"Home" theme** (it returns at the end).

**SCENE 3 — The fog lifts (0:28 – 0:40)**
- **VISUAL:** Dawn. Karl stands at the firing slit and peers into the thick sea fog. The fog slowly lifts and reveals a horizon packed with hundreds of brick-built ships. Karl's eyes widen; he steps back and grips his rifle. A low hum grows — bombers appear in the clouds.
- **CAMERA:** Over-the-shoulder through the slit; rack focus from Karl's face to the fleet.
- **SFX:** Silence… then a rising multi-engine drone.
- **MUSIC:** Drone swells, slow taiko hits begin.
- **➜ TRANSITION:** Karl shouts silently and slams the alarm bell; the camera whips up into the sky toward the bombers.

---

### ACT 1 — "THUNDER FROM THE SKY" (0:40 – 2:10)

**SCENE 4 — The alarm (0:40 – 1:00)**
- **VISUAL:** Germans scramble from bunks into the trenches. Otto mounts the MG-42 at the slit. Karl shakes Hans awake and pushes his helmet onto his head.
- **CAMERA:** Handheld, fast tracking through the trenches.
- **SFX:** Alarm bell, boots on brick floors, rifle bolts, shouts (unintelligible, buried in the mix).
- **MUSIC:** Staccato strings, tempo rising (~110 BPM).

**SCENE 5 — Bombs fall (1:00 – 1:30)**
- **VISUAL:** B-17s open their bomb bays high above. Bombs fall in slow motion. Along the cliffs, explosions throw bricks, sand and studs into the air. A trench wall collapses — Hans is buried up to his chest. Karl claws the bricks away with his C-shaped hands and pulls him free just before another blast.
- **CAMERA:** "Bomb-cam" following one bomb down; then a low, shaking ground camera in the trench.
- **SFX:** Bomb whistles, ground-shaking explosions with sub-bass, bricks rattling down, tinnitus ring.
- **MUSIC:** One second of silence before the first impact → huge drum hit.

**SCENE 6 — Naval bombardment and strafing (1:30 – 2:10)**
- **VISUAL:** Far out at sea, a battleship fires its huge guns. Shells rip into the coast. Dust pours from the bunker ceiling; Otto covers the candle with his helmet. Two **P-51 Mustangs** roar along the cliff strafing the trenches; Karl drags Hans into the bunker as bullet lines kick up sand behind them. A single German fighter streaks past, chased by "LUCKY LADY."
- **CAMERA:** Slow motion on the muzzle blast; inside-bunker shaking camera; low angle as P-51s pass overhead.
- **SFX:** Massive cannon booms, incoming-shell scream, ceiling dust hiss, .50-cal bursts, Merlin engine scream.
- **MUSIC:** Full hybrid orchestra, dark and heroic (~130 BPM).
- **➜ TRANSITION:** The bombardment stops. Silence. Karl looks through the slit — the camera pushes through it toward the sea.

---

### ACT 2 — "HELL ON THE BEACH" (2:10 – 4:40)

**SCENE 7 — They are coming (2:10 – 2:35)**
- **VISUAL:** Through the slit: dozens of Higgins boats race toward the beach. Otto loads a fresh belt into the MG-42. Hans trembles. Karl puts his hand on Hans's shoulder, then aims his rifle.
- **CAMERA:** POV through the slit; tight close-ups of hands, ammo belt, eyes.
- **SFX:** Distant boat engines growing louder, waves, heartbeat.
- **MUSIC:** Low pulse and ticking percussion — maximum tension.

**SCENE 8 — The ramps drop (2:35 – 3:05)**
- **VISUAL:** Ramps fall. Otto opens fire; glowing brass casings pour out like a waterfall. On the beach, American minifigures dive behind hedgehog obstacles; some pop apart and tumble (no blood). Karl fires, works the bolt, fires again — then his hands slow down. He is shaking.
- **CAMERA:** Inside the bunker down the MG barrel; slow motion of casings; close-up of Karl's gritted teeth.
- **SFX:** Thunderous MG-42 echo in concrete, rifle cracks, casings tinkling, distant shouts.
- **MUSIC:** Enemy-side theme: low brass ostinato, then strings rising in dissonance.

**SCENE 9 — Mercy (3:05 – 3:30)**
- **VISUAL:** Karl's scope finds a young American — **Tommy** — stuck in the surf, his rifle lost, freckled face terrified. He looks just like Hans. Karl's finger rests on the trigger… he lowers the rifle. Through the smoke, an American medic (**Doc**) drags Tommy behind an obstacle.
- **CAMERA:** Scope POV; slow push-in on Karl's eyes; cut between Hans and Tommy's faces (match cut).
- **SFX:** All sound muffles into a low drone and heartbeat.
- **MUSIC:** A single fragile piano phrase from the "Home" theme.
- **➜ TRANSITION:** A huge explosion snaps the sound back to full volume.

**SCENE 10 — The breach (3:30 – 4:00)**
- **VISUAL:** A Bangalore torpedo blasts a gap in the barbed wire below. American soldiers led by **Jack** charge up the bluff. Otto swings the MG to stop them; bullets spark off the bunker. Jack reaches the bunker wall and pulls a grenade pin.
- **CAMERA:** Wide shot of the breach; reverse angles between Otto at the slit and Jack climbing.
- **SFX:** Bangalore explosion, Thompson bursts, bullets smacking concrete.
- **MUSIC:** War drums, ~140 BPM.

**SCENE 11 — The bunker falls (4:00 – 4:40)**
- **VISUAL:** The grenade bounces into the bunker. Otto shoves Karl and Hans into the back tunnel and turns toward the grenade. Flash. Smoke fills the tunnel. Karl looks back: Otto's helmet lies on the floor beside the silent MG-42. Karl picks up the helmet for a moment, then sets it down gently, grabs Hans, and runs through the tunnel into the daylight behind the cliffs.
- **CAMERA:** Slow motion as the grenade lands; long tracking shot through the dark tunnel to the bright exit.
- **SFX:** Grenade clatter, muffled blast, ringing ears, footsteps echoing in the tunnel.
- **MUSIC:** Cut to silence on the blast → slow, grieving string chord.
- **➜ TRANSITION:** Karl and Hans burst out of the tunnel into blinding sunlight — white flash into midday.

---

### ACT 3 — "IRON AND FIRE" (4:40 – 6:50)

**SCENE 12 — Retreat through the hedgerows (4:40 – 5:05)**
- **VISUAL:** Midday. Karl and Hans run along a sunken lane between tall brick hedgerows with other retreating German soldiers. A Kübelwagen speeds past. P-51s flash overhead; everyone dives into the hedges.
- **CAMERA:** Low tracking shot behind them; top-down shot of the lane.
- **SFX:** Heavy breathing, boots on dirt, distant artillery, aircraft passes.
- **MUSIC:** Driving, tense ostinato.

**SCENE 13 — The village (5:05 – 5:30)**
- **VISUAL:** They reach a ruined French village with a church tower. German soldiers set up defenses. A little French girl hides in a doorway; Karl sees her and quietly gestures for her to go to the cellar. She runs. Then Shermans and American infantry appear at the end of the street.
- **CAMERA:** Slow tracking past broken houses; close-up of the girl's eyes; snap zoom to the Shermans.
- **SFX:** Church bell swinging in the wind, birds, then the clanking of tank tracks.
- **MUSIC:** Suspenseful pizzicato.

**SCENE 14 — Street fight (5:30 – 5:55)**
- **VISUAL:** Firefight erupts. Karl and Hans shoot from a window. A Sherman's shell blows the church tower apart. Bricks rain down; Karl pulls Hans under a doorway as the wall collapses.
- **CAMERA:** Fast cuts through windows and doorways; slow motion of the collapsing tower.
- **SFX:** Rifle and SMG fire, Sherman 75 mm cannon, shattering glass, avalanche of bricks.
- **MUSIC:** High-energy hybrid action (~145 BPM).

**SCENE 15 — The Tiger (5:55 – 6:20)**
- **VISUAL:** The ground trembles. A wall bursts open and a huge grey **Tiger** tank rolls through. Its 88 mm gun turns and fires — a Sherman's turret sparks and it backs away. German soldiers cheer. Karl does not; he looks at the burning village.
- **CAMERA:** Low hero angle on the Tiger emerging from dust; slow turret rotation.
- **SFX:** Deep Maybach engine rumble, squealing tracks, ear-splitting 88 mm crack, metal ricochet.
- **MUSIC:** Heavy low brass with distorted bass.

**SCENE 16 — Fire from the sky (6:20 – 6:50)**
- **VISUAL:** Green smoke rises from the American lines. "LUCKY LADY" dives out of the sun and fires rockets; a Sherman flanks at the same moment. The Tiger explodes, its turret flipping into the air. The blast throws Karl and Hans to the ground. When the smoke clears, they are alone in the ruins, surrounded. Hans is hurt in the leg and cannot walk.
- **CAMERA:** Parallel cutting between cockpit and street; ultra slow motion of the explosion; then a quiet wide shot of the two small figures in the rubble.
- **SFX:** P-51 dive scream, rocket whoosh, giant explosion, ammo cooking off — then ringing silence.
- **MUSIC:** Biggest climax of the film, cut to silence on the explosion.
- **➜ TRANSITION:** Slow fade through smoke to the warm orange light of sunset.

---

### EPILOGUE — "STEEL DAWN" (6:50 – 8:00)

**SCENE 17 — Surrender (6:50 – 7:15)**
- **VISUAL:** Sunset. American soldiers move through the ruins. Karl sees them. He looks at his rifle, at Hans — then ties a white cloth to the bayonet, raises it, and steps out with his hands up. **Jack** aims his Thompson at him. A long, tense moment. Jack lowers the gun.
- **CAMERA:** Silhouette against the sunset; slow push-in on both faces.
- **SFX:** Wind, crackling fires, distant church bell.
- **MUSIC:** Strings hold one long, tense note.

**SCENE 18 — The medic (7:15 – 7:35)**
- **VISUAL:** Karl points to Hans. **Doc** kneels and bandages Hans's leg. Behind Doc stands **Tommy** — the young American Karl spared on the beach. They recognize each other. Tommy gives a small nod.
- **CAMERA:** Gentle close-ups; shallow depth of field.
- **SFX:** Bandage tearing, Hans's breathing calming.
- **MUSIC:** "Home" theme returns on piano.

**SCENE 19 — Two photos (7:35 – 7:52)**
- **VISUAL:** Sitting on the rubble, Jack takes the photo of his daughter from his helmet strap and looks at it. Karl takes out the photo of his mother and sister. They show each other their photos. No words — just a tired half-smile. The little French girl comes out of the cellar and places a flower on Otto's helmet, which another soldier has carried from the beach.
- **CAMERA:** Two-shot at golden hour; close-up of the two photo tiles side by side; close-up of the flower.
- **SFX:** Wind through grass, birds returning.
- **MUSIC:** Solo trumpet with soft strings.

**SCENE 20 — Final shot (7:52 – 8:00)**
- **VISUAL:** Karl and Hans sit on the back of an American truck as it drives toward the sea. Karl looks at the sunset over the fleet. The camera rises high over Normandy as P-51s fly into the sun. Fade to black: *"In memory of everyone who never came home."*
- **SFX:** Truck engine, a single distant plane fading away.
- **MUSIC:** Final emotional swell, then fade out.

## 4. SOUND DESIGN GUIDE
| Category | Sounds |
|----------|--------|
| Aircraft | B-17 drone, P-51 Merlin scream, dive whine, doppler flybys |
| Weapons | Kar98k bolt and shot, MG-42 buzzsaw, MP40, Thompson, M1 Garand "ping", .50 cal, ricochets, casings |
| Explosives | Bomb whistle and impact, naval guns, shells, grenades, Bangalore, 75 mm and 88 mm cannon, rockets |
| Vehicles | Higgins boats, Sherman, Tiger, Kübelwagen, truck |
| Environment | Waves, wind, dripping bunker, candle, church bell, birds, collapsing bricks (add a light plastic "clack" to falling bricks for the toy feel) |

**Mixing:** sub-bass on every big explosion; one second of silence before key impacts (Scenes 5, 9, 11, 16); muffled low-pass sound during Karl's moment of mercy.

## 6. PRODUCTION NOTES
- **Resolution:** 1920×1080 or 3840×2160; key action inside the 16:9 safe area.
- **Brick look:** glossy ABS plastic, visible studs, cylindrical heads with printed faces, C-shaped hands; macro "toy photography" depth of field; smoke and fire can be soft VFX, debris is loose bricks and studs.
- **Color grade:** cold blue for the bunker night, desaturated teal-and-orange for the battle, warm gold for the epilogue.
- **Continuity:** Karl's look never changes except for growing dirt; Hans's oversized helmet; Otto's helmet becomes the memorial prop.
