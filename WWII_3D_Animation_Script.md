### PROLOGUE — "THE CALM" (0:00 – 0:40)

#### SCENE 1 — Black screen / title (0:00 – 0:08)
- **VISUAL:** Pure black. A single heartbeat-like pulse of distant thunder. The title **"STEEL DAWN"** fades in, metal-textured, sparks drifting through the letters. Under it: *"Normandy, France — June 6, 1944"*.
- **CAMERA:** Static, slow push-in on the title.
- **SFX:** Low wind, faint waves, distant rumble of ship engines.
- **MUSIC:** Single deep cello note + low synth drone (tension starts).
- **➜ TRANSITION:** The title letters dissolve into sea spray, which becomes the next shot.

#### SCENE 2 — Deck of the troop ship, pre-dawn (0:08 – 0:28)
- **VISUAL:** Dark blue pre-dawn. A massive fleet stretches to the horizon, silhouettes of hundreds of ships, barrage balloons floating above. On deck, soldiers sit packed shoulder to shoulder.
  - **C1 (Jack)** stands at the rail, takes the photo of his daughter from his helmet strap, looks at it, tucks it back.
  - **C2 (Tommy)** sits with trembling hands, quietly cleaning his rifle. He looks up, scared.
  - **C3 (Doc)** sits beside Tommy, gives him a calm nod and a pat on the helmet, shifts the matchstick in his mouth.
- **CAMERA:** Wide establishing drone shot over the fleet → slow dolly along the deck → close-ups on the three faces (shallow depth of field).
- **SFX:** Waves slapping the hull, creaking metal, rope clinks, coughing, ship's horn far away.
- **MUSIC:** Soft, emotional piano melody over the drone (the "Brotherhood" theme — reprised at the end).

#### SCENE 3 — The sky wakes up (0:28 – 0:40)
- **VISUAL:** Jack hears something and looks up. A low hum grows. The men slowly raise their heads one by one. On the horizon behind them, dozens of tiny dark shapes appear in the clouds: **bombers**.
- **CAMERA:** Low angle from behind Jack's shoulder, tilting up to the sky; rack focus from his helmet to the planes.
- **SFX:** Rising multi-engine drone of B-17s (radial engines), growing louder until it shakes the frame.
- **MUSIC:** Drone swells; percussion begins (low taiko hits, slow tempo).
- **➜ TRANSITION:** A B-17 roars directly over the ship, filling the screen; the camera "rides" upward with it into the clouds.

### ACT 1 — "THUNDER FROM THE SKY" (0:40 – 2:10)

#### SCENE 4 — Inside the B-17 cockpit (0:40 – 0:55)
- **VISUAL:** Golden first light breaking through clouds. **C5 (Lt. Novak)** grips the yoke, sweat under his oxygen mask. The co-pilot points ahead: the French coastline. Through the windshield, a vast formation of bombers in tight boxes.
- **CAMERA:** Inside cockpit, handheld vibration; then exterior shot pulling back from the nose to reveal the whole formation.
- **SFX:** Deafening engine drone, rattling cockpit, intercom static clicks, wind whistling.
- **MUSIC:** Brass enters — heroic but tense, tempo rises (~110 BPM).

#### SCENE 5 — Flak barrage (0:55 – 1:10)
- **VISUAL:** Black puffs of **88mm flak** burst around the formation. One bomber takes a hit to its engine — fire and black smoke trail behind it. Shrapnel pings the cockpit glass of Novak's plane, cracking it.
- **CAMERA:** Fast cuts: exterior orbit around the formation, POV through the cracked glass, close-up of Novak's eyes.
- **SFX:** Sharp *KRUMP* flak explosions, metal shrapnel rain, engine sputtering, alarm bell.
- **MUSIC:** Strings stab in short staccato bursts.

#### SCENE 6 — Dogfight (1:10 – 1:40)
- **VISUAL:** German **Bf-109 fighters** dive out of the sun toward the bombers. Suddenly, **P-51 Mustangs** scream in from above. **C4 (Capt. Holloway)** in "LUCKY LADY" rolls hard, locks onto a Bf-109, and fires. Tracers streak across the sky; the 109 bursts into flames and spirals down. A second 109 gets on Holloway's tail — bullets rip his wing. He pulls a tight vertical loop, flips behind the enemy, and shoots it down. He gives a thumbs-up to the bomber crew as he flies past Novak's window.
- **CAMERA:** Dynamic chase-cam behind planes, barrel-roll camera spins, slow-motion as the 109 explodes, gun-camera POV with crosshair.
- **SFX:** Merlin engine scream of the P-51, high-pitched dive whine, .50 cal machine-gun bursts (*brrrrt*), explosions, bullets tearing metal, wind rush on rolls.
- **MUSIC:** Full orchestra + electric hybrid percussion, fast and heroic (~130 BPM). Hit points synced to each kill.

#### SCENE 7 — Bombs away (1:40 – 1:55)
- **VISUAL:** Bomb bay doors open. Rows of bombs drop out in slow motion, whistling down toward the German coastal defenses. Cut to ground: German soldiers looking up in horror as bombs land — a chain of massive explosions rips across the cliffs, bunkers, and trenches.
- **CAMERA:** Camera follows one bomb falling from the bay all the way to impact (single continuous "bomb-cam" shot), then wide aerial of the explosion chain.
- **SFX:** Bomb bay door mechanism, falling bomb whistle (descending pitch), ground-shaking explosions with sub-bass, debris raining down.
- **MUSIC:** Music cuts to silence for 1 second right before impact → massive drum hit on the explosion.
- **➜ TRANSITION:** Smoke from the explosions fills the screen; the camera pushes through the smoke and emerges over the sea.

#### SCENE 8 — Naval bombardment (1:55 – 2:10)
- **VISUAL:** Battleship **USS *Texas*** turns its huge turrets. The 14-inch guns fire — a giant shockwave flattens the water around the ship. Shells fly overhead toward the coast. Below, hundreds of **Higgins boats** race toward the beach, white wakes behind them.
- **CAMERA:** Low angle on the gun barrels, slow motion of the muzzle blast; then a sweeping aerial down from the battleship to the landing craft below.
- **SFX:** Enormous cannon *BOOM* with pressure-wave rumble, shells whistling overhead, boat engines buzzing.
- **MUSIC:** Brass motif repeats, then shifts to a pounding, darker rhythm.
- **➜ TRANSITION:** The camera dives down toward one specific Higgins boat — the squad's boat.

### ACT 2 — "HELL ON THE BEACH" (2:10 – 4:40)

#### SCENE 9 — Inside the Higgins boat (2:10 – 2:35)
- **VISUAL:** Cramped, soaked, rocking violently. **C7 (Coxswain Lee)** at the wheel. Soldiers vomit over the side. Tommy squeezes his harmonica, eyes closed. Jack checks his Thompson, then looks at each of his men and raises two fingers — "stay together". Doc pulls his medic bag tight. Bullets begin pinging off the front ramp: *tink, tink, tink*.
- **CAMERA:** Handheld, tight, claustrophobic. Water splashes onto the "lens". Close-ups of hands, eyes, the ramp.
- **SFX:** Diesel engine roar, waves crashing, seasick groans, distant explosions, bullets ricocheting off steel.
- **MUSIC:** Music drops to a low heartbeat pulse and ticking clock-like percussion (maximum tension).

#### SCENE 10 — The ramp drops (2:35 – 2:55)
- **VISUAL:** The ramp slams down. Instantly, MG-42 fire rakes the boat — soldiers in front are hit (no gore — they fall out of frame). Jack shouts silently (no dialogue, just mouth movement) and leaps over the side. Tommy and Doc follow.
- **CAMERA:** Static shot from the back of the boat looking at the ramp as it falls — revealing the burning beach. Then the camera plunges **underwater** with Jack.
- **SFX:** Ramp chain clank and slam, MG-42 "buzzsaw" ripping fire, shouts.
- **MUSIC:** A massive orchestral hit as the ramp drops, then silence.

#### SCENE 11 — Underwater (2:55 – 3:08)
- **VISUAL:** Muffled underwater world. Bullets streak through the water leaving bubble trails. Jack pulls Tommy, who is sinking under the weight of his pack, cuts the strap with a knife, and drags him up.
- **CAMERA:** Slow motion underwater, light rays from above, bubbles.
- **SFX:** Muffled, deep underwater sound; bullets "zipping" through water; heartbeat.
- **MUSIC:** Ethereal choir/pad, slow.
- **➜ TRANSITION:** They break the surface — sound and chaos explode back at full volume.

#### SCENE 12 — Omaha Beach chaos (3:08 – 3:35)
- **VISUAL:** The beach is a nightmare: burning vehicles, Czech hedgehog obstacles, smoke, sand fountains from mortars. Jack, Tommy, and Doc sprint from obstacle to obstacle. A mortar lands nearby, throwing Tommy to the ground; Doc drags him behind a hedgehog and checks him — he's okay. Jack points to the **seawall** 50 meters ahead.
- **CAMERA:** Long tracking "oner" shot (single continuous take) following the trio at knee height through explosions.
- **SFX:** Constant MG-42 fire, rifle cracks, mortar whistles and explosions, sand pelting helmets, tinnitus ringing after the near-miss.
- **MUSIC:** Driving war drums return, aggressive tempo (~140 BPM).

#### SCENE 13 — The bunker (3:35 – 3:55)
- **VISUAL:** Cut to inside the German bunker. **C8 (MG-42 gunner)** fires through the slit, shell casings pouring out like a waterfall. Through his view: soldiers falling on the beach. His loader feeds a new belt.
- **CAMERA:** Inside-bunker POV down the barrel; close-up on his scarred face lit by muzzle flashes; slow motion of glowing shell casings.
- **SFX:** Thunderous MG-42 inside concrete (echoing), casings clinking on the floor, German commands (unintelligible shouting only).
- **MUSIC:** Dark low-brass ostinato (the enemy theme).
- **➜ TRANSITION:** Match cut — the MG-42 barrel swings toward the camera → cut to Jack's face looking right at the bunker from the seawall.

#### SCENE 14 — The plan at the seawall (3:55 – 4:10)
- **VISUAL:** The squad and other survivors are pinned behind the seawall. Barbed wire blocks the way. Jack grabs a **Bangalore torpedo** from a fallen engineer, signals with hand gestures: Tommy covers, Doc stays with the wounded. Jack and two extras slide the Bangalore under the wire.
- **CAMERA:** Tight over-the-shoulder shots, hand-signal close-ups, sand trickling from the wall from bullet impacts.
- **SFX:** Bullets smacking the wall, wounded groans, the metallic slide of the torpedo tube.
- **MUSIC:** Rising string tremolo, tension builds.

#### SCENE 15 — Breach and assault on the bunker (4:10 – 4:40)
- **VISUAL:** BOOM — the Bangalore blasts a gap in the wire. Jack charges through, sprinting up the bluff in zig-zags. Tommy, shaking, stands up and fires his Garand at the bunker slit — the "ping!" of the empty clip ejecting. His covering fire forces the gunner down for a moment. Jack reaches the bunker side, pulls the pin on a grenade, counts with his fingers (3… 2… 1…) and throws it into the slit. Explosion — smoke and fire blast out of the slit. The MG-42 falls silent.
- **CAMERA:** Wide shot of the breach explosion; low-angle running shot behind Jack; slow-motion of the grenade flying into the slit; reverse shot from inside the bunker as the grenade lands at C8's feet.
- **SFX:** Bangalore explosion, Garand shots + iconic *ping*, grenade pin pull, muffled bunker explosion, then sudden quiet as the MG stops.
- **MUSIC:** Climax — full orchestra + choir, then cut to one held note as the gun goes silent.
- **➜ TRANSITION:** Jack, on top of the bunker, raises his arm and waves the men forward. Hundreds of soldiers surge past the camera up the bluff → camera rises into a wide aerial of the beach, with the time-lapse sun climbing higher.

### ACT 3 — "IRON AND FIRE" (4:40 – 6:50)

#### SCENE 16 — Shermans roll ashore (4:40 – 5:00)
- **VISUAL:** Midday. Landing ships open their doors and **M4 Sherman tanks** roll out onto the beach. **C6 (Sgt. Cruz)** stands in the hatch of "IRON MAIDEN". Jack's squad climbs onto the back of the tank. Tommy plays a few notes on his harmonica; Doc smiles for the first time.
- **CAMERA:** Low ground-level shot as the tank tracks crush past the lens; then a dolly shot of the squad riding the tank.
- **SFX:** Heavy diesel/gasoline engine growl, clanking tracks, sand crunching, a few notes of harmonica.
- **MUSIC:** Brief warm, hopeful version of the "Brotherhood" theme.

#### SCENE 17 — Bocage country / the village (5:00 – 5:25)
- **VISUAL:** The column moves through Normandy hedgerows into a ruined French village with a church tower. Quiet. Too quiet. Jack spots a glint in the church tower — **sniper**! A shot hits the tank's armor near Tommy's head. The squad jumps off and takes cover.
- **CAMERA:** Slow tracking shot past broken houses, a French flag hanging from a window; a POV through the sniper scope targeting Tommy; snap zoom back to Jack spotting the glint.
- **SFX:** Birds, distant church bell swinging in the wind, tank idle — then a sharp sniper rifle crack and ricochet.
- **MUSIC:** Music thins to a suspenseful pizzicato.

#### SCENE 18 — Street fight (5:25 – 5:50)
- **VISUAL:** German infantry open fire from windows. A firefight erupts in the street. Jack leads the squad house-to-house. Cruz turns the Sherman's turret and blasts the church tower — the sniper's position collapses in dust. A German soldier rushes Tommy from an alley; Jack knocks him down at the last second. Doc runs under fire to drag a wounded soldier to safety.
- **CAMERA:** Fast-paced cuts, camera moving through windows and doorways, slow-motion of the tower collapsing.
- **SFX:** Thompson SMG bursts, Garand, Kar98k bolt action, MP40 fire, glass shattering, Sherman 75mm cannon blast, stone crumbling.
- **MUSIC:** High-energy hybrid orchestral action track (~145 BPM).

#### SCENE 19 — The Tiger appears (5:50 – 6:10)
- **VISUAL:** Suddenly the ground trembles. Dust shakes off rooftops. At the end of the street, a wall explodes outward — a massive **Tiger I tank** breaks through. **C9 (Tiger commander)** lowers his binoculars. The Tiger's 88mm gun turns slowly toward IRON MAIDEN. It fires — the shell bounces off the Sherman's turret in a shower of sparks. Cruz's crew is shaken.
- **CAMERA:** Low hero angle on the Tiger emerging through dust (villain reveal); slow menacing turret rotation; close-up of the 88mm barrel's muzzle.
- **SFX:** Deep rumbling Maybach engine, squealing tracks, wall collapse, the ear-splitting crack of the 88mm, metal ricochet, ringing.
- **MUSIC:** Enemy theme returns, heavier, with distorted bass.

#### SCENE 20 — Sherman vs. Tiger + air support (6:10 – 6:50)
- **VISUAL:** IRON MAIDEN reverses behind a building. Cruz signals to Jack. Jack throws a **smoke grenade** in the street — green smoke billows (a signal for air support). Cut to the sky: **Capt. Holloway** (C4) in LUCKY LADY spots the green smoke, rolls into a dive. Meanwhile, the Sherman races around the block to flank the Tiger. The Tiger's turret turns too slowly. Holloway fires rockets — they hit the Tiger's engine deck; flames erupt. At the same moment, IRON MAIDEN fires into the Tiger's side armor. The Tiger explodes, its turret blown into the air. Holloway pulls up and passes low over the village, wagging his wings. The soldiers cheer silently, raising their rifles.
- **CAMERA:** Parallel editing between ground and air; cockpit POV in the dive; slow-motion of rockets leaving the rails; ultra-slow-motion of the Tiger explosion with the turret flipping; low-angle of the P-51 roaring overhead.
- **SFX:** Smoke grenade hiss, P-51 dive scream, rocket launch *whoosh*, huge explosion, ammunition cooking off, Sherman cannon, cheers (muffled, music-forward).
- **MUSIC:** The biggest musical climax of the film — the main heroic theme with full choir, brass, and drums. Cut to silence on the final explosion, then a triumphant final chord as the P-51 passes overhead.
- **➜ TRANSITION:** The P-51 flies toward the sun; lens flare whites out the screen → fades into the warm orange sunset.

### EPILOGUE — "STEEL DAWN" (6:50 – 8:00)

#### SCENE 21 — Sunset on the hill (6:50 – 7:20)
- **VISUAL:** Golden sunset over the village and the distant sea, where the fleet still floats. Smoke rises gently. Jack, Tommy, and Doc sit on a hill. Doc bandages Tommy's arm. Tommy pulls out his harmonica — it has a bullet dent; it saved his life. He shows it to Jack; they laugh quietly. Jack takes out the photo of his daughter, smiles.
- **CAMERA:** Slow, wide, peaceful shots; gentle close-ups; warm color grading.
- **SFX:** Wind through the grass, distant planes, crackling small fires, birds returning.
- **MUSIC:** Soft piano reprise of the "Brotherhood" theme, with strings.

#### SCENE 22 — The fallen (7:20 – 7:40)
- **VISUAL:** Jack walks to a row of rifles planted bayonet-down in the earth with helmets on top — makeshift memorials for the fallen. He removes his helmet and bows his head. Tommy and Doc join him. Behind them, a French girl from the village places a flower beside one helmet. Jack sees her — she reminds him of his daughter. He gently hands her a small chocolate bar from his ration.
- **CAMERA:** Silhouette shot against the sun; slow push-in on the helmets; close-up of the flower.
- **SFX:** Silence except wind; one distant church bell rings.
- **MUSIC:** Solo trumpet (taps-inspired melody, original composition) over soft strings.

#### SCENE 23 — Final shot (7:40 – 7:52)
- **VISUAL:** The three soldiers stand up, put their helmets on and walk toward the horizon with the column of tanks and troops moving inland. The camera rises into the sky, showing the endless Allied army moving forward, a squadron of P-51s flying over in formation into the sunset.
- **CAMERA:** Crane up → huge aerial wide shot.
- **SFX:** Rising tank and plane engines, footsteps.
- **MUSIC:** Final swell of the main theme, triumphant and emotional, ending on a long held chord.

#### SCENE 24 — End title (7:52 – 8:00)
- **VISUAL:** Fade to black. Text appears: *"In honor of all who fought for freedom."* Then **"STEEL DAWN"** logo.
- **SFX:** A single distant plane engine fading away.
- **MUSIC:** Final note fades out.

## 4. SOUND DESIGN GUIDE

| Category | Sounds needed |
|----------|---------------|
| **Aircraft** | B-17 radial engine drone (multi-engine), P-51 Merlin engine scream, Bf-109 dive whine, flyby doppler passes, cockpit rattle, intercom static |
| **Weapons** | Thompson SMG, M1 Garand + "ping", Kar98k bolt action, MP40, MG-42 buzzsaw, .50 cal aircraft guns, sniper crack, ricochets, shell casings |
| **Explosives** | Aerial bomb whistle + impact, 88mm flak bursts, naval 14-inch guns, mortars, grenades, Bangalore torpedo, tank cannon (75mm & 88mm), rockets |
| **Vehicles** | Higgins boat diesel, Sherman engine + tracks, Tiger Maybach engine + track squeal, ramp chains |
| **Environment** | Waves, wind, sand impacts, underwater muffled ambience, burning fire, collapsing stone, church bell, birds, tinnitus ring |

**Mixing tips:**
- Use **sub-bass (LFE)** for every big explosion and cannon blast.
- Use **dynamic contrast**: brief total silence before key impacts (Scene 7, 10, 15, 20) makes them hit harder.
- **Underwater (Scene 11):** low-pass filter at ~400 Hz on all sound.
- No voice-over; soldiers' shouts are either muffled or buried under the mix so the story stays purely visual.

## 6. PRODUCTION NOTES (16:9)

- **Resolution:** 1920×1080 (Full HD) or 3840×2160 (4K); keep all key action inside the 16:9 safe area.
- **Color grading:** Desaturated teal-and-orange for battle scenes; cold blue for the dawn prologue; warm gold for the epilogue.
- **Visual effects:** Volumetric smoke, dust particles, camera shake on explosions, lens dirt and water droplets on the beach, motion blur on planes.
- **Continuity:** Keep each character's appearance (face, helmet, gear, signature detail) identical in every shot; dirt and damage should increase progressively through the film (clean on the ship → soaked and sandy on the beach → dusty and bandaged at sunset).
- **Pacing:** Average shot length ~2–4 s in action scenes, 5–8 s in calm scenes.
